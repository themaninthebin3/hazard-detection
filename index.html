What this is

Click anywhere in the world (or draw an area) to pull live flood, wildfire, terrain, earthquake, heat, wind and storm signals for that spot — built entirely from free, public data sources.

  • Flood & river data — UK Environment Agency (England only)
  • Wildfire weather, heat, wind — Open-Meteo forecasts
  • Terrain / landslide — Open-Meteo elevation + soil moisture, ISRIC soil type
  • Earthquake — USGS Earthquake Catalog
  • Hurricane / typhoon / cyclone — GDACS global disaster feed
  • Volcano — Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program, GDACS (current status)
  • War / conflict — curated list, cross-checked against current conflict trackers

Educational tool, not an official hazard assessment. For anything that matters — buying property, insurance, safety planning — check your national flood, geological and meteorological agencies directly.
